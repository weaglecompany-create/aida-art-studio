A New BeginningSome things in life don’t end. They become the beginning of something new.

For me, the phoenix is about renewal and becoming. Rising through the swirling night toward the moon, the bird is a reminder to trust that something beautiful is ahead. About the artist

Aida Auyelbekova

I'm a painter from Kazakhstan, now living and working in Boca Raton, Florida. I have been drawing since childhood and trained professionally as a painter in 2019.

I love animals, so almost every painting I create has an animal in it. I love working with a palette knife: it gives a painting texture and a completely different way of seeing it.

Pop art is where my ideas come fast. When a picture flashes in my head, I put it on canvas right away in bright acrylics. Oil is the opposite: slow and thoughtful. I can let a painting rest for a while and come back to it with a new idea, new strength and new emotions.

My paintings have found their homes in private kindergartens, restaurants and private collections in Kazakhstan. In my first two years in Florida, my work has already found collectors in the United States too, and I also host acrylic masterclasses here.
